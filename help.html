Telengard: Reforged

How to Play

A quick guide to surviving the dungeon and winning the game.

Your Goal Descend through all 20 dungeon floors, grow powerful enough to survive the guardians, defeat the Star Warden on Floor 20, and recover the Star of Telengard. Claiming the Star completes the expedition.

The basic loop

1. Explore. Tap an explored walkable tile on the dungeon map and your adventurer will automatically walk toward it. On desktop you can also use WASD or the arrow keys.
2. Find the stairs. Each floor contains a way down. Descending takes you deeper into the dungeon and increases the danger.
3. Survive encounters. Monsters can appear while you move. Fight, cast spells, guard, drink a potion, or try to flee.
4. Gain XP and level up. Defeating monsters earns experience. Every level makes you tougher and unlocks 4 new spells.
5. Prepare for guardians. Major bosses appear deeper in the dungeon. Floors 5, 10, 15, and 20 are important milestones.
6. Reach Floor 20. Defeat the final guardian and recover the Star of Telengard to win.

Combat

Attack

A reliable physical attack based mainly on your Strength and weapon.

Power

A heavier physical strike that deals more damage.

Spellbook

Choose from every spell you have unlocked. Level 1 begins with 4 spells, Level 2 unlocks 4 more, and so on. Higher-level magic costs more mana but is much stronger.

Guard

Creates protection against incoming damage.

Potion

Restores health during a fight. Potions are limited, so save them for dangerous encounters.

Flee

Attempts to escape a normal monster. Bosses cannot be escaped.

Magic & leveling

You begin at Level 1 with four Level 1 spells. When you reach Level 2, you keep those spells and gain four Level 2 spells. Unlocks are cumulative all the way to Level 20.

Higher spell tiers become progressively stronger in damage, healing, barriers, control, and dungeon-revealing effects. Mana cost rises with their power.

The Arcanist is the strongest pure magic user. Vanguard and Wayfinder characters can still use the same spell progression.

What you will find

Town & equipment

Gold can be spent in the Town Refuge on healing potions, weapon improvements, armor improvements, and full recovery. Stronger equipment becomes increasingly important as monsters scale up on deeper floors.

Death & game modes

Adventurer mode: defeat returns you to your most recent checkpoint with some penalties, allowing the expedition to continue.

Classic mode: death ends the run.

Random dungeons

Every new expedition creates a fresh randomized set of 20 dungeon floors. Choosing Continue preserves the exact dungeon and exploration progress from your saved expedition.

Good first-run strategy

Explore nearby rooms before rushing downstairs so you can collect gold, equipment, and recovery opportunities.
Use lower-cost spells against weaker monsters and save expensive magic and potions for bosses.
Visit town before a difficult descent if your health, mana, armor, or potion supply is low.
Watch the Chronicle while moving—the latest dungeon events appear there.
